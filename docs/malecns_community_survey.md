# MaleCNS community survey — M1e, 2026-09-14

Research only: no community code executed and no parameters adopted. Source snapshots are pinned below; local clones are ignored under `results/malecns/community/`. These are project authors' implementations and self-reported results, not independently reproduced findings. “Not found” means not located in the inspected model, README and results reports, not a claim that an experiment never happened. Parameter inventory covers neural engines and their declared defaults/additions, not world-rendering, locomotor-decoder or unrelated learning-task settings. Optional mechanisms and fitted values are separated from the default model.

## Scope, parameters and outcomes

The comparison baseline is [Shiu's reference `default_params`][shiu]: rest/reset −52 mV, threshold −45 mV, membrane/synaptic time constants 20/5 ms, refractory 2.2 ms, delay 1.8 ms, weight 0.275 mV, Poisson defaults 150/0 Hz, multiplier 250, 1 s and 30 trials. Its [Poisson implementation][shiu-drive] injects voltage directly and removes refractory on selected cells. A rate, duration or trial-count change for an assay is not a newly calibrated membrane constant.

## Added mechanisms and optional settings

- **Hypnagogia:** base noise is off (Gaussian sigma0; background Poisson rate0, N1, weight0.275 mV). Separate stages add noise for offline activity; global gains below1 are labelled deviations, not defaults. Stage12 scans adaptation tau200/600/2000 ms and increment0.05/0.15/0.5/1.5 mV; the follow-up uses200 ms/0.5 mV. Stage13 depression settings are retained fractions0.78/0.75/0.72 with recovery893/1566/2400 ms and alternative synapse scopes. The authors distinguish measured constants from assumed scope; these are not changes made to their stage0 taste assay ([base][hyp-base], [stage findings][hyp-stages]). Graded APL uses tau_syn/t_refr scaling and clipped normalised depolarisation, rather than a fitted gain ([graded implementation][hyp-graded]).
- **FLYCNS:** adaptation tau100 ms, spike increments30 mV for GF/DNp01 and10 mV for `cb_motor` (not VNC motors); GF amount fitted to1–2 spikes, motor adaptation motivated by avoiding refractory-ceiling rates. Electrical delay0.8 ms; GF→TTMn/PSI spikelets9 mV; JO-B1_a/c→GF compound target3 mV at150 Hz; coupling0.2 of leak. These are added connections/mechanisms, not EM edges. Default class gains1, SEZ gain1 (earlier fitted2 withdrawn), baseline depolarisation/noise0, peptide scale0; optional AstA gain0.5/tau2 s. Empty superclass overrides retain core constants. Their reported sweeps (weight0.25–1, class gains up to3, SEZ1.5–4, baseline3–6 mV) are **not permitted variants for us merely because they were tried elsewhere** ([parameters][ion-model], [overrides][ion-graph], [failed interventions][ion-mechanism]).
- **TheMrRaGe:** default APL surrogate off (`apl_w=0`; tau20 ms); older apl_gain200/apl_tau0.35/KC-target0.05 fields explicitly marked inactive. Default background hold0, synapse saturation0, CT1 scale1 and KC→KC scale1 are disabled/unchanged diagnostics. Optional vision uses receptor90 Hz and lamina hold0.72; optional global short-term depression U0.08/recovery480 ms was selected by habituation tests. Optional plasticity adds KC/DA traces1200/600 ms, learning0.0005 per step, floor0.2, trace scales40/20 and DA baseline4000 ms. KC normalisation and threshold1.5 target sparse coincidence coding; MBON hold0.85 offsets otherwise silent output. All are project choices or fits, not transferred Shiu values ([full parameter declarations and reasons][rage-model]).
- **Mehrantsi** retains neural constants, but the live world uses a separate deterministic fractional-rate sensory interface and engineered feeding-context/decoder thresholds; these are not the Poisson Phase0 assay ([world contract][mehran-world], [calibration][mehran-taste]). **Fly.ai** rescales tonic drive when dt changes to retain its resting voltage; incoming weights divide by total absolute postsynaptic input, not by one global density ratio ([engine][alex-model], [normalisation][alex-build]).

## Does each project offer a structural rationale for us?

1. **Hypnagogia:** yes, separating measurement-related synapse-count scaling from external drive and modelling identified graded cells are distinct candidate rationales, but its0.581 number and cell corrections require primary-source and circuit-specific validation before adoption.
2. **FLYCNS:** potentially, explicit fast-transmitter exclusions and intrinsic/electrical mechanisms are structural hypotheses; its fitted global0.3 scale is a sweep result, not an admissible rationale by itself.
3. **TheMrRaGe:** potentially, identified-cell sign and graded-release mismatches offer testable hypotheses; its fitted gains, thresholds and sensory clamping are not automatic corrections to our network.
4. **Mehrantsi:** yes, retaining unsupported-transmitter neurons while omitting their outgoing fast effects is a distinct sign-policy hypothesis; unchanged constants do not demonstrate that our full gate set would pass.
5. **Fly.ai:** postsynaptic normalisation and removing sensory feedback are distinct structural changes, but this different engine is not evidence for transplanting its numerical settings into Shiu's equations.
6. **Philshiu:** the reference supplies fidelity checks, not an independently validated male structural modification.

These are unranked research possibilities, not authorisations. Any future variant follows the [pre-run rationale and complete-gate rule](malecns_phase0.md#further-variant-rule-and-m1d-authorisation--2026-09-14).

Our unscaled L10331 sugar200 **mean129.8 Hz** is of the same order as the **approximately105-Hz median visually read from S17**; the failures concern our additional low-dose/bitter gate conditions, not a demonstrated failure of that published sugar200 operating point.
